2. Principle: no data collection

FairSeat does not process any personal data on the provider's servers. The app works entirely offline. There are no user accounts, no registration, no analytics or tracking services, no advertising and no sharing of data with third parties.

3. What is stored on the device

All entries remain exclusively in the local storage of your device. The following is stored:

  • the classes you create and the names you enter
  • optionally the levels (A, B or C) you assign to individual names
  • rules on who should always or never sit next to each other, and optionally seating wishes (such as “near the board” or “quiet spot”)
  • groups you create yourself or take over from FairMix, with name and colour, and the partner history from FairMix, if you import it
  • optionally your timetable (subjects with colours, times, breaks and the links to classes, rooms and seating plans)
  • optionally assessments per class and subject (title, date, type, weighting and the grades, points or smileys entered per pupil), for class tests optionally the tasks with their maximum points, the grading key and the points scored per task
  • optionally pupils' self-assessments (question, date and the smiley chosen), stored separately from your assessments and never included in averages
  • for class tests, optionally seat numbers and the version A/B per seat, and your notes for a cover teacher
  • rooms with their furnishings (board, doors, windows, furniture) and the seating plans with the assignment of names to seats, including seats you keep fixed
  • optionally earlier seating arrangements, so that new neighbours are formed
  • optionally, in lesson mode, attendance per day and short notes on individual names, the missed lessons counted from them per timetable period, and lessons marked as cancelled, optionally late arrivals (date, period, minutes) and which days of absence are excused
  • optionally, a course log per class (date, period, subject, topic and homework – no names)
  • optionally, lasting observations on individual names (date, subject and a short text) and parent contacts (date, kind such as meeting or phone call, and a short note)
  • optionally, per subject, the flag “taught to different targets” – without any reason or diagnosis – so that such tests are left out when the grade distribution is checked
  • optionally, checklists (title, date and who has already been ticked off) and tally lists such as “homework forgotten” (title and, per name, the days with a mark)
  • for every entry the time of its last change, and for up to 400 days the internal identifiers of deleted entries (no names or contents) – so that backups from two devices can be merged without deleted entries coming back
  • the time of your last backup, so that the app can remind you
  • after loading a backup, a copy of the previous state for 14 days, so that loading the wrong file can be undone
  • your settings for language, colour mode and features

This data is stored using the browser technology localStorage in the protected app directory. The app itself never transmits it; files only leave the device when you share them yourself (see section 5). Uninstalling the app removes it completely. Within the app, names, classes, rooms and seating plans can be deleted at any time; deleted items stay in the app's trash for 30 days and are then removed automatically.

4. Note on entering the names of others

If you enter the names of pupils, you process personal data on your own responsibility. As this data does not leave the device, no transmission to the provider takes place. Please nevertheless observe the requirements of your school and the applicable state regulations on data protection in schools. We recommend using first names or initials only.

Levels (A, B, C) are neutral markers used to seat neighbours mixed or evenly matched. You alone decide what they mean; the app makes no assessment. Levels appear neither on the seating plan nor in full-screen view or printouts. Notes, observations and seating wishes are meant for teaching and organisational matters – please do not enter health data (Art. 9 GDPR), diagnoses or support needs. For pupils taught to different targets there is only the per-subject flag “taught to different targets” – without free text, reason or type of support. If you attach a performance-related meaning to the levels, please check whether the requirements of your school and your federal state permit this on this device.

For teachers: approval by the head teacher

Teachers who process pupils' personal data on a private device often need written approval from their head teacher – in Lower Saxony, Germany, for example, under the ministry's decree on processing personal data on teachers' private IT systems. Please obtain this approval before entering real names and follow the conditions attached to it. Usual conditions are:

  • a device lock with PIN, passcode or biometrics – in addition, FairSeat can lock assessments and observations with Face ID, fingerprint or the device PIN
  • encrypted backups – FairSeat offers a password when saving a backup (AES-256-GCM; the key is derived from the password and never stored)
  • as little data as necessary, e.g. first names or initials instead of full names
  • assessments (grades) only insofar as your approval covers them – they can be hidden entirely in the settings
  • deleting data that is no longer needed, e.g. old classes at the end of the school year

The controller under the GDPR for this data is the school or the teacher, not the provider of the app: the provider receives none of this data and cannot see it.

5. Permissions

The app requests no permissions for camera, microphone, contacts, location or telephony. If you turn on the optional lock for assessments and observations, the app uses your device's unlock function (Face ID, fingerprint or device PIN). The check is done by the operating system; the app only learns whether unlocking succeeded. The app neither processes nor stores biometric data. When you export a PDF, an image, an attendance list, a table (CSV or Excel, e.g. assessments, absences, task analysis, course log), a backup or a transfer file for another device, the file is placed in a temporary app directory and passed on via your device's share function. Where the file goes from there is entirely up to you. When importing (a backup or a FairMix backup), the app only reads the file you select yourself.

6. Internet connections

The app does not establish any network connections of its own. It contains no external libraries, no fonts from third-party servers and no embedded content.

7. Distribution via an app store

When you download the app from an app store, its operator independently processes data (such as your account, device and the time of download). The provider of this app has no influence over this. The privacy terms of the respective operator apply.

8. Your rights

You have the rights to information, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection under Art. 15 to 21 GDPR, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. As no personal data is stored by the provider, requests for information or erasure addressed to the provider will generally have no subject matter. You can delete all data yourself in the app at any time.
